FAQ

Clarity before the first case.

Short answers to the questions teams usually ask about bounded recovery.

What is AI Revenue Recovery?

It is an AI-assisted platform for detecting failed payments, diagnosing likely causes, recommending interventions, validating them with deterministic guardrails, and measuring confirmed outcomes.

Does AI directly control payments?

No. AI is advisory only. The guardrail engine controls authorization, and only approved actions reach the recovery execution boundary.

What happens when a payment fails?

The failure enters the payment workflow, becomes a recovery case, is diagnosed, receives a recommendation, passes through policy checks, and is tracked through any action and outcome.

What are guardrails?

Deterministic merchant policies covering retry limits, recovery windows, duplicate prevention, high-value escalation, fraud and stopping conditions, with fail-safe behavior.

Which businesses can use it?

The product is designed for SaaS, subscription, e-commerce, gym and fitness, EdTech, membership, and B2B recurring-revenue businesses.

Does it work without an external AI provider?

Yes. The local deterministic provider keeps development and simulation workflows working when external credentials are unavailable.

How is recovery measured?

Recovery analytics use persisted payment, recovery-case and confirmed recovery-result records rather than case status alone or estimated values.

How is merchant data isolated?

Authenticated merchant views enforce server-side workspace scoping for dashboards, cases and related records.

Can teams review recovery history?

Yes. Each merchant-scoped case exposes diagnosis, recommendation, guardrail decision, action, result and audit timeline.
